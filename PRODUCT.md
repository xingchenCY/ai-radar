# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI developers, product managers, content creators, and interview reviewers who need to scan reliable AI updates quickly. They arrive with limited time, often between work sessions, and want a clear shortlist without losing source traceability.

## Product Purpose

AI Radar aggregates public RSS sources, separates original publish time from collection time, and presents bilingual AI updates in a fast, readable interface. Success means a user can understand what changed today, verify sources, and filter to the items worth reading in about three minutes.

## Brand Personality

Precise, editorial, calm. The product should feel like a compact intelligence desk rather than a noisy AI dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid purple AI gradients, glassmorphism, dark hacker dashboards, huge empty hero space, equal white card grids, decorative metrics, fake screenshots, stock imagery, and vague marketing copy.

## Design Principles

Show the feed first. Brand expression supports scanning, it does not delay the task.

Make trust visible. Every article keeps source, language, publish state, and original link close to the title.

Use hierarchy instead of decoration. Priority comes from scale, spacing, order, and restrained accent color.

Keep filters close to reading. Search, date, source, language, and topic controls should feel like an editor toolbar.

Handle imperfect data honestly. Unknown dates, stale snapshots, and failed sources remain visible without panic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, 44px mobile touch targets, reduced-motion support, and no dependence on color alone for critical state.
